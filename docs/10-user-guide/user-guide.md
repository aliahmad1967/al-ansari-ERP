# User Guide

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 First Login
1. Launch AL-ANSARI ERP application
2. Enter username and password
3. Select language (Arabic/English)
4. Click "Login"

### 1.2 Dashboard
After login, you see the dashboard with:
- KPI widgets (employee count, pending approvals, etc.)
- Recent activity feed
- Quick action buttons

- All content switches immediately

## 3. Common Tasks

### 3.1 Searching
- Use the search bar at the top of list pages
- Search is debounced (results appear as you type)
- Supports partial matches

### 3.2 Creating Records
1. Navigate to the module list page
2. Click "Create" or "+" button
3. Fill in the form
4. Click "Save"
5. Success toast appears

### 3.3 Editing Records
1. Navigate to the record detail page
2. Click "Edit" button
3. Modify fields
4. Click "Save"
5. Changes are saved immediately

### 3.4 Deleting Records
1. Navigate to the record
2. Click "Delete" button
3. Confirm deletion in dialog
4. Record is soft-deleted (can be restored)

### 3.5 Exporting Data
1. Navigate to list page
2. Click "Export" button
3. Choose format (CSV/Excel/PDF)
4. File downloads to your computer

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I reset my password?
Contact your system administrator via Settings > Users.

### Q: How do I backup my data?
Go to Data Management > Backup. Download the backup file.

### Q: Can I use the app offline?
Yes. AL-ANSARI ERP works fully offline. All data is stored locally.

### Q: How do I switch between Arabic and English?
Click the language toggle in the top header bar.

### Q: Why can't I see a menu item?
You may not have permission. Contact your administrator.

### Q: How do I import data from Excel?
Go to Data Management > Import. Upload your Excel file.

### Q: Can I undo a posted journal entry?
No. Posted entries are permanent. Create a reversing entry instead.
